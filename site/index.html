HFE NFC Card Templates

8 background templates for the HFE card. Each is a 1920×1080 PNG that drops straight into Admin → NFC Cards → Templates. The cards below show them on the real card layout (logo + QR on top, name and contact at the bottom).

Names, QR and contact details are sample data. The QR is a placeholder, not scannable.
Background PNGs: backgrounds/ · full card previews: previews/. Dark backgrounds are on purpose: the card text is light, so a light background would make it unreadable.
